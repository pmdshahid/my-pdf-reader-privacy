Privacy Policy for My PDF Reader

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the My PDF Reader mobile application ("the App") collects, uses, and discloses information. This App is developed and published by Shahid ("we", "us", "our").

Information We Collect

1. Your PDF Files

The App lets you open PDF files so you can read them. Your PDF files are:

  • Opened and displayed on your device only.
  • Never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any servers that store your data.
  • Copied into the App's private storage on your device when you open them, so that they appear in your Recent and Favourites lists. Other apps cannot access this private copy. You can remove a file from the list, or clear the whole list from Settings, and the private copies are deleted.

2. Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • The list of PDF files you opened (file name, size, last opened date, favourite status, last page read)
  • App settings (theme and night mode preference)

This data stays on your device. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted. We do not have access to it.

3. Sharing and Printing

If you choose to share or print a PDF, the file is handed to the app or printer service that you select. We do not receive a copy of it.

4. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use data (such as advertising identifiers and general device information) to show relevant ads and measure ad performance, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can review and adjust your ad personalization settings through your device's Google Settings.

5. Permissions Used

  • Internet: required to display advertisements.

The App does not request access to all of your files. You choose each file yourself using the system file picker, or by opening a PDF with the App from another app.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not require account creation or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
